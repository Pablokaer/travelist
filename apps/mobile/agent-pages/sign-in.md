# Log in to Travelist

Log in to Travelist to open your travel lists, saved walks and trips, on the web or in the iPhone and Android apps.

## Ways to log in

- **Email or nickname** and your password.
- A **magic link**: enter your email and use the link or the 6-digit code it contains, valid for one hour.
- **Google** or **Apple**, where offered.
- **Forgot password?** sends a link to choose a new password.

This page is a form for people: an agent cannot log in on someone's behalf. Send the person to [the log-in page](https://travelist.live/sign-in).

New to Travelist? [Create your free account](https://travelist.live/sign-up) or read about it on the [home page](https://travelist.live/).
