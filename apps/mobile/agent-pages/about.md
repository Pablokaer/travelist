# About Travelist

Travelist helps you prepare for a trip and explore a city on foot: a travel list app and city guide with a walking route planner, on the web and in the iPhone and Android apps.

Travelist is built on open data. Every place, photo and text comes from an open source and keeps its licence and credit.

## Data sources

- Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
- Attraction data from Wikidata (CC0)
- Images from Wikimedia Commons (licence shown per image)
- Popularity from Wikipedia pageviews (Wikimedia)
- City descriptions from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0, linked on each city page)
- Map tiles by OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles
- Walking routes © openrouteservice.org by HeiGIT
- Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0)
- Visa data from the passport-index-dataset (MIT)
- Travel advisories from the Government of Canada (Open Government Licence – Canada)
- Exchange rates from Frankfurter (European Central Bank) and ExchangeRate-API
