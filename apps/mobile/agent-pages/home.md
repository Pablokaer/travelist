# Travelist — turn any city into your personal travel list

Travelist is a free **travel list app** and **city guide** for the web, iPhone and Android. Discover the best places in cities across Europe, North Africa, Asia and Brazil, save the ones you want to visit to your own lists, see them on a map and follow a **walking route** between them.

[Create your free account](https://travelist.live/sign-up) · [Log in](https://travelist.live/sign-in) · [About and data sources](https://travelist.live/about)

## What you can do with Travelist

- **Explore any city:** top attractions, viewpoints, parks, museums and hidden gems, with photos, opening hours, ratings and reviews from other travellers.
- **Create your lists:** save the places you want to visit and organise them your way.
- **See it on the map:** your list on a map, with the most efficient walking route between the stops.
- **Plan the walk:** a walking route planner that orders your stops, shows the distance and time on foot and opens the route in Google Maps or Apple Maps.
- **Prepare the trip:** a pre-trip checklist personalised to your passports, covering visas, passport validity, power plugs, weather, money and safety.
- **Read the story of each city:** its history and introduction from Wikipedia, famous people and walk lists shared by other travellers.

## How it works

- **Choose a city:** pick a destination across Europe, North Africa, Asia and Brazil.
- **Discover places:** browse the top sights on the map or in a list.
- **Build your list:** add the places you love, in any order.
- **Follow your route:** walk the shortest route and open it in Google Maps or Apple Maps.

## Popular destinations

Paris, Rome, Tokyo, Barcelona, London and Lisbon, among nearly two hundred cities.
