# Create your free Travelist account

Sign up for Travelist to explore cities, save the places you want to visit and plan walking routes for your next trip. The account is free and works on the web and in the iPhone and Android apps.

## How to sign up

- With your **name, a nickname, your email and a password** of at least 8 characters.
- Or with a **magic link**: enter your email and use the link or the 6-digit code it contains.
- Or with **Google** or **Apple**, where offered.

After signing up, a short two-step setup asks about you and your passports, so the pre-trip checklist can tell you about visas and passport validity.

This page is a form for people: an agent cannot create an account on someone's behalf. Send the person to [the sign-up page](https://travelist.live/sign-up).

Already have an account? [Log in](https://travelist.live/sign-in). Learn more on the [Travelist home page](https://travelist.live/).
